## Featured builds

### 🛡️ GIGO — Offline scam intelligence with a physical Sentinel

A South African hackathon MVP that detects suspicious messages, links and email content offline, explains the risk in plain language, and turns governed cyber alerts into a physical Arduino response.

- Offline-first 0–100 scam and fraud detection
- South African bank and public-service intelligence
- Expo mobile experience for iOS and Android
- Arduino Uno Sentinel with OLED, LEDs, buzzer and physical acknowledgement
- Authorised localhost Nmap and honeypot presentation sequence
- Firebase identity, private sync and governed AI agents
- Premium-ready UX at R59/month or R499/year
